# Acceptable use policy

AppstackX Ltd · Owner: Khuram · Approved 11/11/2026 · Next review 11/11/2027

Applies to everyone working for AppstackX and to every device or account used for company work.

## Devices
1. Company work only on **approved devices**: today, Khuram's Mac and iPhone (listed in the Cyber Essentials scope). A
   personal device used for company email or consoles is **in scope** and must meet these rules. A device belonging
   to another employer (e.g. a day-job laptop) is **never** used for AppstackX work.
2. Every device must have:
   - a **supported OS**, with **automatic updates on**; security updates are installed (and restarted for) within
     **14 days** of release;
   - **full-disk encryption** (FileVault on the Mac; a passcode on the phone);
   - **screen lock** after 5 minutes at most, unlocked by password, 6+ digit PIN or biometrics;
   - **firewall on** (Mac) and malware protection active (XProtect/Gatekeeper or a named anti-malware product; App
     Store apps only on phones);
   - a **Standard (non-admin) daily account**; admin credentials are used only to install or change software.
3. Install only software that is needed, supported and from the official store or vendor. Remove what is unused or
   unsupported. No jailbroken or rooted phones.
4. A lost or stolen device is reported to Khuram **at once**. It is then locked and wiped remotely (Find My) and
   treated as an incident.

## Accounts and passwords
Unique passwords from the password manager, MFA on every account, no shared logins, and never reuse a work password
anywhere else (see [access control](access-control.md)).

## Handling data
- Patient and clinic data stays **inside the product** (the clinic's own area). It is not copied to email, chat,
  spreadsheets, personal cloud storage, screenshots or AI/chat tools. Support is done with the clinic's permission and
  only to the extent needed.
- Use **fictional data** in demos, tests, videos and marketing.
- Do not put personal data into URLs, file names or logs.
- Prospect files and third-party forms are kept out of git and deleted when no longer needed.
- Send personal data only to known, checked recipients. Double-check the address before sending.

## Email and web
- Be suspicious of unexpected links, attachments, invoices and "urgent" requests, especially requests to change bank
  details, share codes or approve sign-ins. Check them through a known contact. Report suspected phishing (forward to
  report@phishing.gov.uk) and treat any click on one as a possible incident.
- Do not browse or read email while signed in as an administrator.

## Working away from home
Avoid public Wi-Fi for admin work; use the phone's hotspot instead. Lock the screen when you step away, and keep
screens out of view in public places.

## Leaving
Return or wipe company data from devices, hand over access, and accounts are closed the same day.

Breaking this policy may lead to access being removed. Report concerns to khuram@appstackx.co.uk.
