**Background**

- A. AppstackX provides ClinForms (clinforms.co.uk). ClinForms completes the report forms that referrers send to a
  clinic (medico-legal companies, rehabilitation and case-management providers, insurers, solicitors and employers),
  in each referrer's own layout, from the clinic's treatment notes. The clinic's clinicians review, change and approve
  every form.
- B. Blue Heart uses ClinForms under [TO CONFIRM: the ClinForms terms at clinforms.co.uk/terms and the order dated
  [date]] (the **"Agreement"**). This DPA forms part of the Agreement. It sets out the terms that Article 28 of the
  UK GDPR requires.
- C. Blue Heart has its own clinic account in ClinForms (clinic id `blue-heart` [TO CONFIRM]), covering all its sites.
  ClinForms serves several clinics from one database. Each clinic's data is kept separate as Annex 2 describes.

## 2. Roles and scope

- 2.1 For Clinic Personal Data, Blue Heart is the **controller** and AppstackX is its **processor**.
- 2.2 For Users' account data (names, work email addresses, roles, sign-in records, two-step verification settings
  and the activity log), AppstackX is the controller, as the ClinForms privacy policy explains. AppstackX uses it only
  to provide, secure and account for the service. The professional details that appear on approved forms (a
  clinician's name, job title and HCPC number) are part of those forms, so they are also Clinic Personal Data.
- 2.3 Annex 1 sets out the subject matter, duration, nature and purpose of the processing, the types of personal
  data and the categories of data subjects.

## 3. Blue Heart's instructions and responsibilities

- 3.1 **Instructions.** Blue Heart's documented instructions are: the Agreement, this DPA, and the choices that Blue
  Heart's owners and administrators make in its clinic account (for example who is invited and with which role, the
  retention period, which referrer forms are set up, and whether drafting from notes is switched on). Blue Heart may
  give further instructions in writing; an email to the contact in clause 12 is enough [TO CONFIRM].
- 3.2 **Blue Heart is responsible for:**
  - (a) having a lawful basis (UK GDPR Article 6) and a condition for health data (Article 9, and Schedule 1 of the
    Data Protection Act 2018 where one is needed) for processing its patients' information in ClinForms and for
    sending completed forms to referrers [TO CONFIRM with Blue Heart's data protection lead or solicitor];
  - (b) telling its patients, in its own privacy notice, that a supplier prepares referrer reports from their notes,
    including the drafting service and the locations in Annex 3;
  - (c) the accuracy of its notes, and uploading only what is needed for the forms it chooses to complete;
  - (d) checking that its contracts with referrers allow this processing. Some referrers' terms require notice before
    data leaves the UK; one such clause was found in HCML's published supplier terms (clause 5.4) [TO CONFIRM by
    Blue Heart for each referrer];
  - (e) managing its Users: inviting only people who need access, removing leavers promptly, and making sure each
    person uses their own account;
  - (f) not sending patient information to AppstackX by email or any other channel outside ClinForms, unless both
    parties agree a channel for a specific support case;
  - (g) deciding when to switch drafting from notes on (it is off when the clinic account is created).
- 3.3 **Clinical responsibility.** Approved forms are the work of the clinician who approves them. ClinForms is a
  documentation tool. It does not give clinical advice or make clinical decisions.

## Annex 2 – Technical and organisational measures

Built and live on clinforms.co.uk since 10 October 2026 unless marked otherwise.

**A. Access**
- Accounts by invitation only; public sign-up is switched off. Clinics are set up by AppstackX; Blue Heart's owners
  and administrators invite their own Users.
- Passwords of 12 to 128 characters, stored only as a one-way hash.
- **Two-step verification is required** for every User (an authenticator app code, plus 10 single-use backup codes).
  It cannot be switched off and devices cannot be "trusted". Ten wrong codes lock the second step for 15 minutes. No
  clinic data is available before two-step verification is set up.
- Roles: owner, administrator, clinician, staff. Only a clinician, administrator or owner marked "may sign" and with
  an HCPC number can approve a form. Only owners and administrators can delete approved reports.
- Sessions last 12 hours. Users can sign out other devices; removing a User or a session takes effect at once.
- Sign-in rate limits; addresses and IP addresses in those counters are stored only as keyed hashes.
- Invitation links expire after 7 days and work once; password reset links expire after 2 hours. Until the email
  service is switched on, the administrator who creates a link passes it on.

**B. Separation of clinics**
- Every request is checked against the signed-in User's clinic, and every database query is limited to that clinic.
- Each clinic's data is encrypted with its own key (section C), and each encrypted value is bound to its clinic,
  table and record, so a value moved to another clinic or record cannot be decrypted.

**C. Encryption**
- HTTPS for all connections; HTTP Strict Transport Security for 2 years, including subdomains.
- Reports, referrer form set-ups and form files are encrypted in the application before they are stored, with
  AES-256-GCM and a key specific to each clinic (derived with HKDF-SHA256 from a master key). Form files are stored
  in encrypted chunks.
- Not encrypted by the application, because they hold no patient information: ids, statuses, form titles, referrer
  names, stored file names (always neutral, such as `form.pdf`), settings, clinic and member profiles, and the
  activity log.
- Master keys are held only in the hosting provider's encrypted environment settings, in AppstackX's secrets file
  (readable by its owner only) and in an encrypted offline copy. They have been rotated once, before any clinic data
  existed.

**D. Data minimisation before drafting**
- Drafting is **off** for a new clinic until an owner or administrator switches it on. While it is off, nothing is
  sent to the drafting service (forms are read with ClinForms' own layout rules).
- Before notes are sent to the drafting service: the patient's name is replaced by a placeholder (swapped back by
  ClinForms afterwards), age is sent instead of date of birth, addresses and contact details are left out, and dates
  of birth in the note text are removed.
- Names, dates of birth, references and clinician details on the form are filled in by ClinForms from the record,
  never by the drafting service.
- For employer and case-manager forms, past medical and social history are held back.
- When a filled-in form is uploaded as a template, its patient details are masked before the form's questions are
  read, and PDFs are sent blank.
- Staff can see exactly what would be sent before drafting.
- Note and form text is treated as data, never as instructions.

**E. Approval and integrity**
- A form can be downloaded as final only after a clinician has approved it. The approval is bound to the exact
  content approved (a signed fingerprint), so any later change needs a new approval.
- Each drafted answer links to the notes it came from; unanswered questions are flagged for the clinician rather than
  guessed; opinions such as prognosis or fitness for work are included only when a clinician recorded them.
- Approval is blocked while any mail-merge field from a template is left unfilled.

**F. Logging and audit**
- An append-only activity log (the database refuses changes and deletions) records sign-ins, two-step verification
  changes, member and role changes, clinic detail and settings changes, partner key changes, form set-up, notes
  imports (format and counts only), drafting, approvals, final downloads, exports and deletions. It never holds
  patient information.
- Owners and administrators can read the whole clinic's log and download it; clinicians and staff see their own
  entries.
- Application logs hold ids, timings and counts only, never note text, names or secrets.

**G. Retention and deletion**
- Retention period set by the clinic (30 to 3,650 days); a daily job deletes reports not changed for that period
  [TO CONFIRM: first run on the live service to be verified before real patient data].
- Abandoned uploads are deleted after a day. Offboarding exports and then deletes the clinic's data in one step
  (clause 9).

**H. Backups and recovery**
- The database provider's point-in-time recovery (7 days on the current plan; 30 days on the paid plan).
- [Planned, before real patient data: a scheduled encrypted off-site backup, TO CONFIRM.]

**I. Application security**
- Security headers: frame blocking, no content-type sniffing, strict referrer policy, restricted browser
  permissions. The Content Security Policy runs in report-only mode [planned: enforcement].
- The database is reached only through a gateway that checks a signature on every request (valid for 60 seconds),
  accepts only ordinary data statements and refuses schema changes.
- Uploaded files are checked for size and structure; encrypted PDFs are refused; previews of Word files are
  sanitised.
- Product analytics run only with consent and never include patient information.

**J. Organisational**
- One administrator with production access (clause 4.3); secrets are never stored in source code.
- Clinic accounts are created only for clinics that have signed a DPA.
- Support requests are handled without patient information unless a channel is agreed (clause 3.2(f)).
