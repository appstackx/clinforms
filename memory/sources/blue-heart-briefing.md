# Blue Heart Clinics: briefing for the call with Dell (06/10/2026)

**Legend:** [C] = seen in a public source, [I] = our reasoning or estimate, [U] = unknown. Every fact here comes from search-engine snippets. The proxy blocked direct page reads; I re-tested Companies House and blueheartclinics.co.uk today and both are still blocked.

## Identity check: it is Dell's business (high confidence)
- **Both researchers found the same business.** Same company number (09158709), same brand, same freephone (0800 093 1899), same Aylesbury address and the same list of 11 clinics. [C]
- **Dell is the director and owner.** Companies House names **Mr Dell David Henson-Baines** as director of BLUE HEART CLINICS LTD. The website names "Dell" as Founder & Owner. That "Dell Baines" is this person is [I], high confidence.
- **Corrections to the researchers' notes:**
  - The Dunstable registered office (48 High St South) is a working clinic. The job ad gives Blue Heart Clinics at "Ground Floor", so the idea that it is only an accountant's address is dropped.
  - ZoomInfo's "HQ 67 Marlowes, Hemel Hempstead" is the old Physio Assured address. Downgraded.
  - The team is bigger than "at least 9": the team page lists about 16 names. [C]
  - New fact: Dell no longer treats patients and now only runs the business. [C] He is the buyer, not the person who would use the tool, so pitch on clinicians' hours, consistent reports and getting paid faster.
  - The Hornchurch / Edgware job ad (findajob 16735750) was not re-checked, so treat it as the researcher's finding.
- **Do not mix up with:** Blue Heart Care Group (a Welsh care company; its ~$2.1m revenue and 21–50 staff are not this business), Blue Heart Healthcare Solutions Ltd, or Blueheart Ltd.

## Who they are
- **What they do:** private physiotherapy for muscle and joint problems (MSK), plus acupuncture, massage, kinesiology, psychotherapy, counselling and podiatry. [C]
- **History:** started in 2010 as Physio Assured with "one clinic, one therapist". The company was renamed Blue Heart Clinics on 10/01/2023. [C]
- **Professional standards:** physios are HCPC-registered, CSP members and DBS-checked. [C]
- **Who pays:** patients themselves, private medical insurers (AXA Health, Aviva, Vitality, WPA), and personal-injury rehab and case-management networks. [C]
- **Reviews:** 4.9 stars from 88 reviews for the Dunstable listing. [C]

## Their medico-legal and insurer work
- **Evidence [C]:**
  - The website names 8 rehab and case-management partners: 10 Bridge Physio, 3D Rehabilitation, HCML, Physio-Link, PhysioMed, Speed Medical (OPM), The Physio Network and The Treatment Network.
  - Patient testimonials mention referrals after road traffic accidents and "funded treatment".
  - Dell's email says medico-legal companies (MLCs) and insurers send their own forms, and they also have their own Blue Heart template.
- **What they write [I]:** treatment reports (initial assessment, progress and discharge). There is no sign they write expert court reports.
- **Volume estimate [I, low confidence]:**
  - 5–7 full-time physios × 20–30 new patients a month = about 100–200 new cases a month.
  - If 25–50% are funded by MLCs or case managers, that is 25–100 cases.
  - At 2–3 forms per case, that gives **about 50–300 forms a month; my working figure is about 125.**

## What the problem is worth to them [I]
Assumptions: 30–60 minutes per form, clinician cost £30–50 an hour, and our tool cuts writing time by 50–70% (the demo has to prove this).

| Scenario | Current cost per month | Saving per month |
|---|---|---|
| Low: 50 forms × 0.5h × £30 | £750 | £375–525 |
| **Central: 125 forms × 0.75h × £40** | **£3,750** | **£1,900–2,600 (about 47–66 clinician hours)** |
| High: 300 forms × 1h × £50 | £15,000 | £7,500–10,500 |

- **Extra revenue:** each hour saved can also be sold as 2 half-hour appointments, worth roughly £90–120.
- **What MLCs pay per report:** [U], because network rates are not public. Usually the initial assessment report is included in the assessment fee and progress or discharge reports are unpaid admin [I], so time saved goes straight to margin.
- **Reference point only:** MedCo's fixed fee for a physio's whiplash first medical report is £226 from April 2025 [C]. That is a different kind of report from the ones they write.

## What they can comfortably pay
- **Comfort zone [I]:** **£100–300 a month plus a setup fee of up to about £500.** That is under 1% of estimated turnover and similar to clinic software (TM3 starts at about £50–60 a month, from the researcher). A five-figure build fee would be a stretch.
- **Price to quote:** "Practice £199/mo, founding price **£149/mo fixed for 12 months, plus £250 setup**, paid monthly, cancel any time."
  - The 5 included forms should be their Blue Heart template plus the forms from their top 4 referrers.
  - Year 1 costs about £2,040, against a central saving of about £2k+ a month.
- **Why not Starter at £99:** with 11 sites and about 10 clinicians, it undervalues the product.
- **Plan for form fees:** with 8+ networks and 4 insurers they will want 8–15 forms, and £50 per form adds up. Offer a cap, for example "up to 10 extra forms for £300".
- **What would make them say no:**
  - Per-form fees piling up.
  - Annual prepayment or a lock-in.
  - Per-seat pricing across about 16 staff.
  - VAT on top. Physio treatment is VAT-exempt, so they probably cannot reclaim it, and £149 really costs them about £179. Say up front whether prices include VAT [I].
  - Any doubt about patient data: they will need a data processing agreement, UK/EU hosting, no AI training on their data, clinician sign-off and an audit trail.
  - Having to retype notes from TM3.

## Questions for Dell on the call (max 5)
1. How many MLC and insurer forms a month, and which 3–4 referrers send the most? Can he send blank copies plus one anonymised completed example?
2. Who writes the reports and how long do they take? Do late reports hold up approval of more sessions or payment?
3. How do they use TM3: where do the notes live, and is copying or exporting from TM3 acceptable for the first version?
4. Which legal entity do we contract with (the company is filed dormant)? What is their VAT status, and do the MLCs impose any rules on how patient data is handled?
5. How many clinicians would use the tool, who else signs off, and what would a 30-day pilot need to show?

## For the demo video
- **Input:** realistic TM3-style notes for an invented whiplash patient from a road traffic accident.
- **Output:** their Blue Heart template, plus a case-manager initial assessment form and a discharge form, then clinician review and sign-off, PDF export, and the time saved shown on screen.
- **Branding:** use a generic MLC-style layout, not real HCML or Speed Medical branding, until Dell sends their actual forms. Use no real patient data.
