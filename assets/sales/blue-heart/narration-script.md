The on-screen captions already carry the message, so the voice-over can be dropped or shortened without losing anything.

- The TM3 shown is a **Simulated TM3 sandbox – demo data, not affiliated with TM3**. Every patient, clinician and referrer is fictional.
- The form reading and both drafts were produced **live during this recording**. - A direct TM3 connection is subject to TM3 providing access; today's route is a TM3 notes export, uploaded in one step.
- Don't claim time savings – none have been measured yet. Counts on screen are read from the app during the recording.
- Security: describe the measures as what will be in place before any real patient data is used, not as already certified.
